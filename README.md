
# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

## About the Movie

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a superhero movie released in 2021. It is part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) and was directed by *Jon Watts*.

The movie follows Peter Parker as he faces unexpected challenges after his identity as Spider-Man is revealed to the world.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

## Movie Information

**Movie Title:** Spider-Man: No Way Home

**Release Year:** 2021

**Director:** Jon Watts

**Main Character:** Peter Parker

**Actor:** Tom Holland

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction

## Main Characters

- **Peter Parker** – The friendly neighborhood Spider-Man.
- **MJ** – Peter's girlfriend and close friend.
- **Ned Leeds** – Peter's best friend.
- **Doctor Strange** – A powerful sorcerer who helps Peter.

I like this movie because:
- Spider-Man is one of my favorite superheroes.
